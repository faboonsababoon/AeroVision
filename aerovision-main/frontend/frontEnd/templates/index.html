About AeroVision

The D Check, a rigorous maintenance procedure for aircraft, traditionally requires up to two months of downtime, 50,000 man-hours, and millions of dollars in lost revenue. Our innovative solution addresses this challenge with an AI-powered damage detection system that leverages Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). By combining precision and efficiency, this technology enables real-time detection of structural damage and predictive maintenance. The result is a significant reduction in inspection times, operational disruptions, and costs, while ensuring enhanced safety and reliability. Developed by a team of passionate high school students for the Conrad Challenge, this solution redefines aircraft maintenance processes and sets a new standard for safety, efficiency, and sustainability in the aviation industry.
